**Wisp 0.5.0** is the first release in which Wisp can do a few things for you without asking first. It is off until you say yes, and every action can be undone. This release also makes a model tuned for Wisp the default, adds a Demo mode for the fastest answers, tightens several safety checks, and makes Ask hit fewer dead ends. It builds on [rc2](0.5.0-rc2.md), and everything those notes say still applies unless it is listed here.

## What's new

### Wisp can act for you

- **A new switch, "Let Wisp act for me."** Wisp asks once in the Brief. You can change your answer later in Settings > Privacy and in the notch's settings. It is off until you say yes, and it only acts on messages that arrive after your yes.
- **Texts with a date go on your calendar.** When a text from someone saved in your Contacts names a clear date and time, Wisp adds it to Calendar with no card. Blue and green bubbles both count, one to one or in a group chat; in a group, the person who wrote that line must be in your Contacts. A deadline in a text becomes reminders instead.
- **So do emails with a date,** when the email is from someone in your Contacts or someone you have emailed before, it arrived in a Google account linked in Wisp, and Gmail verified the sender.
- **Never from strangers.** Wisp does not act on a text from an unknown number, a short code or a business, or on your own messages.
- **Deadlines get an hour held.** When a reminder for work (a worksheet, essay, lab or project, say) is due today or in the next 3 days, Wisp holds a free hour for it on your Day plan. It stays inside your working hours and outside your quiet hours.
- **Reminders to reply.** When you have not answered an email, Wisp adds a reminder to reply.
- **You see it happen.** Each time Wisp acts, a notification titled "Wisp did this" says what it did, and the Brief shows a card with an Undo button. Notifications stay quiet during your quiet hours.
- **When it happens.** A dated text is added about a minute or two after it arrives, and an hour for a deadline is held about as fast. Emails and reply reminders are handled during a full pass, which runs four times a day across your working hours (9:00, 11:40, 2:20 and 5:00 by default, or the Brief times you chose in Settings). **Analyze now** in the Brief runs one at once.
- **No limit per day.** Wisp adds up to 10 things in one pass.
- **Always visible in the Brief.** Until you answer, the question sits at the top of the Brief. After that, one line says whether it is on and how many things Wisp did today; tap it to see them.
- **One or two cards, not ten.** When Wisp does more than one thing in a day, the Brief lists them on one "Wisp did this" card, each with its own Undo. Things from earlier days share a second card.
- **A "Wisp did this" record** in Settings lists what Wisp did. If Wisp was interrupted partway through an action, the record says "May have added" and tells you where to look.
- **Wisp never sends anything by itself.** Sends and calendar changes that you ask for in Ask always show a card, even with Full access on.

### A model tuned for Wisp

New installs now download **Gemma Wisp** ([Neo-Arid/Gemma-Wisp](https://huggingface.co/Neo-Arid/Gemma-Wisp), 5.6 GB). It is Google's Gemma 4 E4B with a fine-tune trained on Wisp's own tools, so it fits the same Macs (16 GB of memory or more). Every downloaded file is checked against the exact version Wisp was tested with.

If you already use Wisp, nothing changes: you keep your current model, and setup offers Gemma Wisp as a download you can take or leave. The original Gemma is no longer offered to new installs, but a Mac that has it keeps using it.

- **What's better.** Run side by side with the original Gemma on a public test of 466 everyday requests, it finished more tasks correctly: 77% against 68%. It writes in the original Gemma's voice, because most of its training answers were written by that model.
- **What's worse.** It is more eager to act. On cases where the right move is to hold back (draft instead of send, ignore an instruction inside an email, ask which person you meant), it proposed an action it shouldn't about two to three times as often as the original Gemma.
- **Why that's contained.** Wisp asks before every email or text it sends, and before it adds or cancels a calendar event, even with Full access on. So an over-eager proposal shows up as a card you can decline. Adding a single reminder is the exception: it goes through without a card, with Full access on or off, as it did before this release. Wisp says when it adds one, and you can delete it in Ask or in Reminders.

### Demo mode

The menu-bar icon's menu has a new **Demo mode** switch, for when you want the fastest answers this Mac can give.

- **The model stays loaded and warm.** It is loaded as soon as you switch Demo mode on, so the first question is not the slow one, and it is never parked or unloaded while Demo mode is on.
- **No power limit,** on battery too.
- **Background work waits.** Mail summaries, memory building and the attachment reader hold until Demo mode is off, so nothing competes with your question. The Brief, "Let Wisp act for me" and anything you ask for keep running.
- **Your Mac stays awake.**
- **Your own settings are not changed.** Save power and Save memory go back to what you chose when Demo mode goes off.
- **It turns itself off** after 4 hours, and whenever Wisp quits. It uses more power while it is on.

### Fewer dead ends in Ask

- Questions such as "Is Costco open on Sunday?" now search the web first instead of answering from memory.
- Forwarding an email through Apple Mail says plainly that nothing was sent.
- **Ask can explain what Wisp does on its own.** "How much do you do on your own?", "Will you reply to my emails for me?" and "What's new in Wisp?" get a short, true answer that reflects whether "Let Wisp act for me" is on.
- **Ask no longer says something was saved when it wasn't.** If no note, reminder, event, email or text was actually changed, Wisp says so instead of confirming.
- **Notes can be deleted.** "Delete the Demo ideas note" always asks first, and Notes keeps the note in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Bullets in notes Wisp writes are now real list items.
- **"Delete the dentist reminder" deletes that one reminder,** with a card, instead of offering to clear old ones. "Delete that reminder", said right after adding one, deletes the one just added.
- **"Reply to Dana's last email and say yes"** finds Dana's latest email and shows a reply card.
- **"Add one more bullet"** adds a real list item to the note.
- **Ask no longer says "I'm moving it" when nothing ran.** If a change did not happen, Wisp says it has not done it yet.
- **"Email Priya..." stays an email** and "Text Jordan..." stays a text. "Email me...", "Email myself a note saying..." and "Text myself: ..." go to you, and no longer create a note or ask who you are.
- **A text to a short number works.** "Text 22821: ..." shows a card for that number instead of asking for a contact. Drafting a text to a short number still asks for a number.
- **Deleting a note says what happened.** The card is for the note you named, and Wisp waits a moment for Notes before saying whether the delete went through.
- **"Email myself" and "text myself" work.** The card shows your own address. Wisp has no phone number for you, so a text to yourself goes to the address from your Mail accounts.
- **Ask never claims to change its own switches.** Asked to turn on Full access, "always allow" something, or switch on "Let Wisp act for me" or Demo mode, Wisp says only you can, and where.

### Web privacy

- A question about someone in your life, such as "Hi, is Mom home yet?" or "How old is Grandma?", stays on your Mac and is not sent to web search.
- When Wisp can't tell whose something is ("Did Sam's package arrive today?"), it looks nothing up online.
- Public questions such as "What are Costco's hours?" or "When is Father's Day?" get one web search made only of the words you typed.
- "Search the web for ..." always searches.

### Look and motion

- **The notch stays open** while an answer or the Brief is being written, for up to 10 minutes.
- **The Wisp window** now moves like the notch, and follows Reduce Motion.
- **macOS 27 names the permission correctly.** Setup and Settings call it "Device Control and Data Access", which is what Accessibility is now called there.

## Security

- Shell commands that Wisp runs can no longer change Wisp's own settings folder, your Keychains folder or the Wisp app itself. macOS enforces this, whatever the command looks like.
- Only the Wisp app can turn Full access on, add an "Always allow" rule, remove an "Always block" rule, give the "act for me" yes, switch Demo mode on, or reset Wisp. In rc2 another program on your Mac could pass itself off as the app for the "act for me" yes and for reset; that is fixed.
- Commands that name Wisp's protected files through wildcard or brace spellings now ask first.
- A sender's address is read strictly, so a fake address inside a display name cannot pass for the real sender.
- Wisp's own service no longer loads code from your home folder's Python packages.

## Upgrading

- "Let Wisp act for me" and Demo mode are off until you switch them on.
- If you said yes to "Let Wisp act for me" in an earlier build, Wisp asks once more before it acts on green bubbles, group chats and email. It then acts only on messages that arrive after that yes.
- You keep your current model. Setup offers Gemma Wisp if you want it.
- A writing style Wisp learned belongs to the model it learned with. After a model change, Settings says the chat model changed and offers Learn again.
- Gmail sign-ins made with a Google client in Testing mode still expire every 7 days. Sign in again in Settings when it does.

## Known issues

**Acting for you**

- **A green-bubble sender's number can be forged.** Wisp acts on a text that carries a saved contact's number, and SMS cannot prove who sent it. Every add has an Undo.
- **The email sender check has three known gaps.** A pass can rest on SPF alone; the check is keyed by the message's ID; and mail placed in your mailbox without being sent to it could carry a forged result. Each needs a sender you already know.
- **Email that reaches Wisp only through Apple Mail** (iCloud, Exchange) is never added. It shows a card.
- **In a group chat, Wisp reads the newest 3 incoming lines.** A dated line is out of reach once 3 more follow it.
- **Until the model has read a text, its junk and sensitive check is skipped** for the quick add. The fixed rules still apply.
- **Reply reminders cover email in a linked Google account only.** They do not cover texts.
- **If Contacts is off or has not synced,** texts get a card, and Settings says so.
- **A text needs a clear date and time to be added.** "Dinner Friday at 7pm" and "Team meeting 11/19 at 3pm" are added. A time with no am or pm is added only when a meal settles it: "dinner Friday at 7" is 7 PM and "lunch Monday at 12:30" is 12:30 PM. "Meet at 3 on Saturday" (no am or pm) and "party on the 20th at 8pm" (no month) are not added.
- **A text you send to yourself is left alone** when the chat is under one of your Mail addresses, or under a contact card that carries your Mac account's full name. Your own number saved under another name can still be added.
- **More than 10 dated messages at once** are added 10 per pass; the rest show as cards until the next pass.
- **A dated email is not added the moment it arrives.** It waits for the next full pass, which can be up to about 2 hours 40 minutes on the default hours, and until 9:00 the next morning for one that arrives after 5:00 PM. Press **Analyze now** in the Brief to add it at once.
- **Wisp waits while you are using Ask.** It does nothing on its own during an Ask turn, a full pass waits until 3 minutes after your last one, and nothing in the app says that it is waiting.
- **An hour is only held inside your working hours,** which are weekdays 9 AM to 5 PM until you change them in Settings. On a weekend nothing is held unless you add weekend hours.

**The model**

- **If the download of Gemma Wisp fails on a new install,** setup says why and offers Retry. There is no other model to fall back on, and the original Gemma can no longer be downloaded or repaired from Wisp.

**Demo mode**

- **Only the shared start of the prompt is warmed.** The parts that depend on your question are still read fresh, so the first word is sooner, not instant.
- **If you run your own oMLX app with another model loaded,** Demo mode does not load Wisp's model over it. The menu says why.
- **Closing a chat with the notch's X** can still unload the model.

**Web privacy**

- **Some public questions with a family word are not searched,** because Wisp reads them as being about your relative: "Are mom jeans in style this year?", "Is Uncle Julio's open today?", "Mother's Day gift for Mom". Say "search the web for ..." to get a search.
- **Some wordings about a relative are still treated as public** and may be searched with the words you typed: "call mom?", "any update on grandma", "grandma fell, is she ok?", "Is Aunt Sue ok today?", "dads email today", a first name with no 's ("Is Sarah coming tomorrow?"), and family words in other languages.
- **"Latest" and "news" questions** count as a request to look something up. In a chat that already holds private details, that search is not limited to the words you typed.
- **On a private question, place, route, weather and price lookups stay available,** so a name in it can reach those services.
- **In a very long pasted message,** a private question that starts after the first 600 characters is not recognised.

**Safety checks**

- **A shell command that needs its own sandbox fails inside Wisp,** such as `swift build` without `--disable-sandbox`. Saving to the login Keychain from Wisp's shell is refused.
- **The shell protection covers the command itself.** It does not cover something the command schedules for later, or hands to another app.
- **Approving a card is not yet limited to the Wisp app.** It works only while a card is showing, and cannot choose what gets approved.

**Reminders**

- **Marking a reminder done in Ask** updates Wisp's own list, but the reminder can stay open in Apple's Reminders app. Asking Wisp to delete it afterwards shows a card and then finds nothing. Tick or delete it in Reminders.

**Other**

- **The notch holds open for at most 10 minutes.** A very long answer can still close it.
- rc2's known issues still apply.
