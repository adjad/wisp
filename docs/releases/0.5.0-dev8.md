**Wisp 0.5.0-dev8** is a development build for testers. It builds on [dev7](0.5.0-dev7.md), and everything those notes say still applies unless it's listed here.

## Fixes

- **Save power stays quiet when the engine can't lift a limit.** If Wisp's built-in engine doesn't answer, or refuses, when Wisp asks it to lift a power limit mid-reply, Wisp no longer asks again (and logs a warning) every second while that reply runs. It waits a minute before asking again, or stops for the rest of the run if the engine refused. No new limit is set in the meantime. Each ask also gives up sooner: it tries at most two connections, and none after one that timed out.
- **Facts from your mail are stricter about whose they are.** "My sister is moving to Denver" or "my brother started at Stripe" no longer becomes a fact about you; "I just started at Stripe" still does. A word like "if" elsewhere in a sentence no longer drops a fact, while "I was working at" and "I wouldn't work at" now do.
- **A look-alike sender can't name a service.** A service is learned only from mail sent by its own domain, so a look-alike address (comc.io for Comcast) no longer counts, and a hyphenated name like T-Mobile now does.
- **Forget everything from mail keeps what you confirmed.** A fact you confirmed that came from somewhere else stays in Memory; only its link to your mail is removed.
- **Mail memory says why it's waiting, and keeps going on battery.** When mail memory pauses (you're using Wisp, other background work is running, or the app isn't open), it now records why, so a pause no longer looks like a stall. On battery it keeps reading at the 10 W background limit; only Low Power Mode or a battery under 30% pauses it.
