# Wisp 0.5.0 demo runbook (Saturday 2026-11-14)

One page. Every prompt below is a candidate to **rehearse on the installed build on Friday**; none was run
against the live app when this was written. Drop any that miss in rehearsal.

**Checked against preview build 4019** (the one installed 2026-11-12, 4d1a48f2) by reading its code, not by
running it: every step below exists in that build. What a later build adds, and 4019 lacks, is listed at the end;
none of it is needed for these steps.

## Before you go on (10 minutes)

- Open Wisp's Permissions page. Calendar, Reminders, Full Disk Access, Microphone, Screen Recording and
  Accessibility must all read Allowed. If Screen Recording or Accessibility does not, press **Start over**, then Allow.
- Sign in to Gmail again in Settings if you use it: the sign-in expires every 7 days and was due about Nov 12.
- Settings › Models: confirm every row shows the model you rehearsed with (see "Fall back to base Gemma").
- Ask one throwaway question so the model is loaded. The first answer after a launch waits for the load.
- Plug in. Keep the lid open: closing it stops Wisp.
- Contacts must be allowed for Wisp, and your friend must be saved there with the number they text from. Wisp adds
  only from a saved contact's own number; if Contacts is off or has not synced since launch, you get a tap card.
- Settings › Privacy › **Let Wisp act for me**: turn it on and confirm **before** the stage text is sent.
  Wisp only acts on messages that arrive after you said yes, so a text sent earlier will not be added.

## 1. Wisp does something without being asked

1. Have a friend who is **saved in your Contacts** text you from the number or address saved on their card, and do
   not open it. A blue or a green bubble both work, one to one or in a group chat (in a group, the person who
   writes the line must be saved). A text from an unknown number is never added:
   `Dinner at Luigi's on Sunday Nov 15 at 7pm, can you make it?`
   Backup wording: `can you come to my recital Sat Nov 21 at 6pm?`
   If a build older than this one was ever switched on, the Brief first shows a card asking about the new things
   (green bubbles, group chats, email): say **yes** to it before the text is sent, or only a one-to-one iMessage acts.
   An email adds an event only with proof (a From line is not proof of who sent it): the account is a Google one
   linked in Wisp, the sender is in your Contacts or someone you emailed before, and Gmail verified the mail.
   Anything less shows a card with an "Add to calendar?" button instead, so use a text on stage.
2. Open the Brief and press **Analyze now** (the button beside the account menu). Do not type in Ask while it
   runs: a question pauses auto-add until the next cycle. A full pass can take a minute or more; talk over it.
3. The Brief leads with a card marked **WISP DID THIS · CALENDAR**, titled "Added <the event> to your calendar"
   (Wisp words the event's name itself, for example "Dinner at Luigi's").
   Open Calendar to show the event is really there.
4. Press **Undo** on the card. The event leaves Calendar, and Wisp will not add that one again.

Rules worth saying out loud: only texts from people saved in your Contacts (never an unknown number), and emails only
from someone you know that Gmail verified; a clear date in the sender's own words; only messages that arrive after you
said yes; at most 10 at a time, with no limit per day; every add can be undone; and Wisp never sends anything by itself,
in any access mode.

Second beat, no message needed: if your calendar or Reminders has work due in the next three days (a title like
"Worksheet 3" or "Project report"), the same Analyze now pins a free hour for it on Day and shows
**WISP DID THIS · DAY PLAN**. Open Day to show the block; Undo removes it. A deadline is held once, ever,
so create a fresh work reminder shortly before going on, after your last rehearsal, and do not use it in
rehearsal. The slot must fall inside your working hours and outside quiet hours.

What will not trigger an add: a message that arrived before you turned the switch on, an hour with no am or pm and no meal word to settle it ("meet Monday at 3", "call me at 5"; "dinner Friday at 7" and "lunch Monday at 12:30" do add), "maybe",
a message you already opened today, a date under 30 minutes away, or an event already on your calendar.
Each stage message works once: after an Undo the same thread is never added again, so use a new sender or
a new wording for a second take.

## 2. Ask with tools

- `What's on my calendar tomorrow?`
- `Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow at 9am` (with Full access on, the packaged default, the reminder is added with no card; with Full access off it shows a confirmation card to press)
- `Find the email from <a real sender> about <a real subject> and summarize it`
- `When is my next meeting with <a real person>?`

Avoid: asking for a table (the notch is too narrow), long multi-part requests, and anything that sends a
message on stage.

## 3. Brief and Day

- Brief: read the one-line summary, open one card, show a reply draft opening for editing (do not send).
- Day: show the agenda, then `Add lunch with Sam tomorrow at noon` in Ask and watch it appear.

## 4. Look at screen and voice

- **⌥⇧L** with a document or web page in front, then ask `What is this about?`
- **⌥⇧Space**, say `What's my next meeting?`, press ⌥⇧Space again to send at once.

## If something stalls

- "Loading model… (Ns)" is normal after a launch or a model change; wait it out, it is a few seconds.
- An answer that hangs past 30 seconds: cancel it and ask again. Background work (Brief building, mail memory)
  slows live answers; do not press Analyze now right before an Ask prompt.
- Nothing responds: quit Wisp from the menu bar and reopen it. Quitting also stops the engine; the first
  answer after reopening waits for the model again.
- The auto-add card does not appear: check the text is still unread and one to one, press Analyze now once
  more, and if it still does not come, show the tap-to-add card that takes its place and say Wisp held back.

## Fall back to base Gemma

Settings › Models: for every row that shows the tuned model, pick **gemma-4-E4B-it-oQ4e-mtp** (the untuned
base), then ask one throwaway question to load it. The tuned model's name contains `r5-step-01800`; if answers on stage look
off, switch to the base and carry on. Rehearse with the model you will present with.

## Not in preview build 4019 (in later builds only)

Nothing here changes a step above. If you rehearse on 4019 you will not see:

- A warning under **Let Wisp act for me** in Settings when Contacts is off or has not synced. On 4019 the only
  sign is that a text gets a card to tap instead of being added: check Contacts yourself (see "Before you go on").
- "May have added …: check Calendar" in the Settings list, for an action that was interrupted mid-write. On 4019
  an interrupted action is simply not listed.
- The reworded card for new actions ("Wisp needs your yes for these") and "On · N actions wait for your yes" in
  the notch settings. You only meet these after updating from a build with fewer actions, not on a first yes.
- "Not now" leaving an earlier yes untouched. On 4019, pressing Not now on the new-actions card restarts the
  "only messages that arrive after your yes" clock. On stage: say yes in Settings and do not touch that card.
- A "Block time?" card can come back on 4019 for a deadline Wisp already holds an hour for. If it does, ignore it;
  the **WISP DID THIS · DAY PLAN** card is the one to show.
- Stricter checks on shell commands that name Wisp's own files. Not part of the demo.
