**Wisp 0.5.0-dev8** is a development build for testers. It builds on [dev7](0.5.0-dev7.md), and everything those notes say still applies unless it's listed here.

## What's new

### Save power without slowing anything else

- **Background work always at 10 W.** Wisp's background work (mail memory, routines, memory capture) now runs the graphics chip at 10 W all the time, plugged in too. Chat replies and your Brief keep their dev7 limits: 15 W on battery, and the Brief at 10 W after 10 minutes away.
- **Other apps aren't slowed.** The limit applies to the whole graphics chip, so Wisp sets it only while other apps use little of it: under 7.5% of its time, about what an app redrawing its window uses. Beside a limited reply, a light app drawing 120 frames a second took at most a tenth of a millisecond longer per frame and missed no frames. Wisp checks right before each limited request and every second while one runs, and lifts the limit within about a second when another app (a game, a video, an export) starts using more, mid-reply too.
- **Your own work isn't slowed either.** Research or voice transcription never runs under a limit, and a chat reply isn't limited while either one is running.
- **A little later on battery, sometimes.** The first limited request after a pause takes about a third of a second longer, while Wisp takes a second, quick look to catch an app that only just started.
- **Built-in engine only.** Only Wisp's built-in engine can lift the limit mid-request and drop it the moment a request ends. If Wisp runs on your own oMLX app, nothing is limited.
