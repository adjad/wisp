**Wisp 0.5.0-rc1** is the first release candidate for 0.5.0, shipped to testers as rc1 beta builds. It builds on [dev8](0.5.0-dev8.md), and everything those notes say still applies unless it's listed here. The chat model is the same untuned Gemma 4 E4B (`gemma-4-E4B-it-oQ4e-mtp`) as dev8.

## What's new

### Look at your screen, and act on pictures

- **Look at my screen.** A new eye button in Ask, beside the paperclip, looks at the window in front or at a part of the screen you drag over. ⌥⇧L does the first from anywhere. You see what Wisp captured, and which app and window it came from, before anything is sent.
- **Private windows stay private.** Password managers and windows Wisp can tell are private browsing are never captured. They're refused as the front window and left out of a dragged region, and Wisp says what it left out. Any other browser window asks you first. Wisp can't tell when a Safari window is private, so check before tapping **Look anyway**.
- **Pictures that lead to actions.** Ask Wisp to *do* something with a picture ("add this to my calendar", "save this receipt as a note", "reply to this") and it reads the picture, then uses its usual tools. What it read is treated like an email from someone else: every change, send or web lookup asks you first, even with Full access, and offers no Always allow.
- **Look needs Screen Recording.** Without it, Wisp tells you how to turn it on and never asks macOS by itself.

### Better answers in Ask

- **Your Brief is part of day and week answers.** "What's coming up", "what's on Friday" and "anything I need to do this week" include dated items Wisp found in your mail, messages and notes, under **Also from your Brief**. They're shown as information, never counted as busy time.
- **Longer memory within a chat.** Earlier parts of a long chat are kept as a short summary, so Wisp can recall up to about 20 exchanges back.
- **Casual chat gets a direct reply**, and Wisp follows the length and format you ask for. Ask it to always answer a certain way and it remembers.
- **Several things in one request.** "Add reminders for milk, eggs and bread" adds all three. Each one still asks you first wherever it did before, and Wisp never sends the same message twice in one request.
- **Replies read the conversation first.** "Draft a response to Sam", "reply to Ed's email" or "answer her text" now reads the messages or mail first, so the draft answers what was actually said. Plain writing, like "an essay on the public response to the policy", stays plain writing.
- **"Actually Saturday" stays a day plan.** After a question about your day, "actually Saturday", "no, Friday" or "I meant tomorrow" plans that day instead of being read as a new question.
- **No more "let me check…" without checking.** If Wisp's answer only promises to look something up, it's asked once to actually do it.
- **Draft messages show their draft card** again.

### Safety

- **Skills and connected (MCP) tools always ask first.** When Ask decides to use a skill action or an MCP tool, you see a card every time, even with Full access, and the card has no Always allow. An MCP tool you've listed as trusted read-only, and that its server marks read-only, still runs without a card.
- **MCP tools whose names suggest they only read still ask**, under Full access too. List a tool as trusted read-only to skip the card.
- **Switching off Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, the firewall, AirDrop or VoiceOver always asks**, even with Full access. An earlier "always allow" no longer covers it. Turning them on, and switching off dark mode, Low Power Mode or Do Not Disturb, work as before.

### Day tab

- **Remove a suggested block**, with **Undo**, and bring back removed suggestions later. Your own events get **Delete event**.

### Read aloud

- **Choose the read-aloud voice** in **Settings › General › Voice**, with a speed slider and a preview. Read-aloud now uses a male voice by default. To go back to the voice set in macOS, choose **Use system voice**.

### Memory and the Brief

- **Voice and picture encoders load only when used.** On Wisp's built-in engine, the parts of Gemma that hear and see (about 950 MB together) stay out of memory until you press the mic, attach a picture or use Look, and leave again after 5 minutes unused.
- **The Brief catches up when Wisp comes back** after a long time away or asleep.
- **The Models page** no longer shows an idle oMLX as using memory.

### For testers

- **Export Chat Debug Log saves every chat since Wisp opened.** It's in **Settings** and in the menu-bar icon's menu. The file goes to your Downloads folder, readable only by your account, and includes tool results such as email text, so check it before sharing. Chats you delete, or clear with **Forget what Wisp learned…**, are left out. With **Debug Mode** on, it also includes the model's full input and output.

## Fixes

- **Wisp reopens after Quit & Reopen.** When you allow a permission that asks Wisp to Quit & Reopen (for example Full Disk Access or Screen Recording), Wisp now comes back by itself after a few seconds. It never reopens after you quit it yourself, or after a logout, restart, shutdown or sleep.
- **"What's new in Wisp" describes this release.** Asking Wisp what's new now answers from these notes instead of dev8's.

## Known issues

- A Safari private window can't be detected by Look; it's captured only after you tap **Look anyway**.
- Using Ask's own "forget" in a chat doesn't yet remove that chat from the debug export until Wisp quits. Deleting the chat, or **Forget what Wisp learned…**, does.
- A version of Gemma 4 E4B fine-tuned for Wisp is still being trained. This release ships the untuned model; the tuned one replaces it only after it matches or beats the current model on every test.
