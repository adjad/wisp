**Wisp 0.5.0-dev7** is a development build for testers. It builds on [dev6](0.5.0-dev6.md), and everything those notes say still applies unless it's listed here.

## What's new

### An easier-to-read Day calendar

- **A list instead of an hour grid.** The Day tab's calendar now lists the rest of your day in time order, one row each. Every row shows when it starts and ends on the left and its full title, so events that overlap no longer squeeze into narrow columns that hide their names.
- **Now is a line.** A red line with the time separates what's over from what's next. An event that's on now says how long is left.
- **Overdue things come next.** Overdue reminders follow the now line (after anything that's on now), marked Overdue. A reminder due later is listed at the time it's due.
- **Free time is named.** A gap between events, plans and calls reads like "Free until 6:30 PM · 25 min" or "Free 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM · 4 h", so you can see where something fits.
- **What's over folds away.** Things that ended in the last couple of hours fold into one Earlier line. Click it to see them.
- **No legend to decode.** Each row says what it is (Calendar, Your plan, Suggested, Needs you), and a colored bar on its left matches. A long day shows its first 8 things and Show more.
