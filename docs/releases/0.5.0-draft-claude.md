# Wisp 0.5.0: the first stable release

Wisp 0.5.0 works on a Mac with nothing installed: no oMLX, no earlier Wisp. A short setup gets you an engine, a model and a first answer, then three optional questions so Wisp knows who it is working for. Everything stays on your Mac. **[CONFIRM: wording depends on the clean-install run on the final build]**

## Highlights

- **Starts from nothing.** The built-in engine starts on its own, a model downloads (Wisp's own Mellum fine-tune on Macs with 24 GB or more, Ling below that), and the first answer works. Setup lives in the notch.
- **Getting to know you.** Tell Wisp what to call you, your goals and how you like replies. It shapes the brief's greeting, what Day ranks first, and your reply tone. Stored only in `~/.moe/profile.json`, never sent to a cloud model. Reopen it with `/about`.
- **A notch-first Wisp.** Wisp starts in the notch with three tabs: **Ask**, **Brief** and **Day**. Quick settings, a chat switcher, slash commands (`/today`, `/memory`, `/research`, `/settings`, `/report`, `/window`, `/new`) and a keep-open pin are all there.
- **Brief and Day.** Brief gives one sentence about your day and four rows (CAL, MAIL, TEXTS, SOON), each opening in place, plus a "Needs you today" line. Day lays out the rest of today hour by hour: your calendar, tasks, Wisp's suggestions and reminders on one clock. Both refresh in the background, and Brief openings and suggestions follow your local time.
- **Calendar actions with a safety net.** Cancel an event with a five-second Undo, or move it with times Wisp has already checked. Read-only calendars never offer Cancel or Move. Moves are verified against Calendar before Wisp says they happened.
- **Plans from your messages.** Wisp can capture future plans as reminders and Calendar events, spotlight today's priorities, and tell you what it caught, with Undo.
- **Evening check-in and weekly goal pulse.** Mark tasks Done, Tomorrow or Drop, and goals On track, Behind or Done.

## Better everyday answers

- Timers, relative reminders, date and time, weather, the brief, maths and conversions, definitions, timed calendar adds, calendar moves, unread-mail summaries, email lookup, and volume and music now take a direct route instead of a model guess. Writes and sends still ask for confirmation. **[CONFIRM: depends on the router reconcile in #273]**
- Weather replies are clearer on errors and ambiguous city names.
- Personal "should I…" questions stay out of web search.
- Mail and Messages summaries read like written summaries, not headers. Promos and mass mail stop counting as "needs you". When Messages access is missing, Wisp says how to fix it.
- Search, reminders and Calendar answers keep their scope: dates, topics, titles and sources are preserved, partial results say so, and failures are shown beside what did work.
- A prompt that doesn't go through says so, and is resent only when it is provably unsent.

## Day and reminders

- Old undated reminders are asked about once: **Keep** or **Delete** (Delete only when you click it, and only if Wisp can confirm it).
- Reminders a week overdue are hidden from Day and say how long they have been overdue. Tasks stop being carried forward after a week.
- Duplicates of hidden reminders no longer appear as Day tasks.

## Reliability and performance

- Backend no longer freezes while recomposing mail.
- Idle chat rendering, refresh writes and model-status polling are reduced, and large tool outputs are released after their previews fold.
- The oMLX prompt cache is bounded (4 GB, with a 7-day stale sweep) and the vision-feature cache is capped at 1 GB.
- The built-in engine's memory guard no longer refuses a model it has just loaded, quitting Wisp always stops the engine, and a missing `~/.omlx/settings.json` no longer stops the service at launch.
- Full access shows only what Wisp's service confirmed. If it can't be read, Wisp says so instead of showing "off".

## Privacy and permissions

- First launch asks only for Notifications, Calendar and Reminders. Screen Recording and Accessibility are requested when a feature first needs them. **Grant More Access…** in the menu bar covers the rest, including Full Disk Access for Mail, Messages and Notes.
- Personal planning questions stay private, with a note on which sources were actually read.
- Local installs can be re-signed so macOS permission grants survive reinstalls (developer builds).

## Changed

- The Daily Summary is composed every morning and evening (8 AM and 8 PM by default), and appears on the Brief tab instead of in chat.
- Memory and Research are sections of the Wisp window. The old separate windows are gone.
- Versions were renumbered down by 1.0. Wisp is not finished, so every release is 0.x.

## Known limitations

- **Calendar Undo:** one Undo case from the #333 review is not fixed. **[CONFIRM against #340 and the final #273 head: some of it may already be covered]**
- **Brief follow-up:** a follow-up finding from the #333 review is not fixed. **[CONFIRM exact wording from #333's final body]**
- This build is not notarized (see First launch).
- On macOS older than 26, oMLX cannot run. Setup says so and points to Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp or a remote model.
- Mellum, the default on 24 GB or more, ignored the identity name on "what's my name?" and answered "who is the CEO of Apple" without searching in testing. **[CONFIRM against the final build]**
- Test counts quoted for gates are minimums (some gates do not report counts).
