# Wisp 0.4.5

> **Folded into 0.5.0.** 0.4.5 was never published; everything below ships in 0.5.0 (see [0.5.0-rc1](0.5.0-rc1.md) and the changelog).

Draft for a future release. Final build qualification and download details are pending.

Wisp brings conversations, daily briefings and planning together in the notch, with more personal guidance and clearer feedback when a request cannot finish.

- **Ask, Brief and Day.** Switch between your conversation, a daily overview and an hour-by-hour timeline. Pin the notch open, return to saved chats and use slash commands for quick navigation.
- **Morning and evening briefs.** Daily summaries run twice a day by default, with catch-up after sleep or reopening Wisp. Expand Calendar, Mail, Texts and upcoming items for detail.
- **Tell Wisp about you.** An optional local profile lets you share your goals, important people and preferred reply style. Review, change or erase it in Memory.
- **Daily and weekly check-ins.** Mark planned tasks Done, move them to Tomorrow or Drop them. Weekly goal check-ins help keep your brief focused.
- **Calendar actions from Day.** Move eligible events through Ask, or cancel with a five-second Undo window. Wisp explains restrictions for read-only calendars and invitations. Changes can notify invitees.
- **A cleaner day plan.** Mass mail and duplicate event notices are filtered from review suggestions. Related reviews share one block, and short timeline items avoid overlapping.
- **More reliable reminders.** Improvements cover multiple dated reminders, quoted titles and all-day dates. Results distinguish confirmed changes, failures and items that were not attempted.
- **Clearer message recovery.** Failed Ask submissions show a notice and preserve your prompt. Automatic retries are limited to cases where Wisp can establish the request was unsent.
- **Answers that show their limits.** Inbox and planning replies better disclose partial or unavailable sources. Search retains useful results alongside errors, and Messages access failures explain the next step.
- **Quick settings and less background work.** Change permissions and brief times, inspect model memory, and open the full settings from the notch. Wisp avoids unnecessary refresh writes, idle model checks and search processing.

## Profile fixes

- Changing your name, reply style or an unchanged list of goals no longer postpones a due weekly goal check-in. Changed goals start a fresh week; answering a check-in starts the next interval. Existing profiles retain their recorded timing.
- A nondefault reply tone or length works even when you leave your name and goals blank. About you recognizes those preferences as saved content and lets you erase them. Untouched default settings still count as an empty profile.

## Remaining acceptance work

Two UI findings still require repair and verification before release: Calendar Undo can disappear when an event ends during the Undo window, and Ask about this can open an older live brief than the one displayed. These are separate from the profile fixes above.

The acceptance campaign uses isolated stores, synthetic source responses and non-sending native contracts. It does not establish real-provider completeness or qualify the final installed application. The separate idle CPU repair and matched app benchmark remain under their existing owner.
