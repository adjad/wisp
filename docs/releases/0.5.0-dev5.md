**Wisp 0.5.0-dev5** is a development build for testers. It builds on [dev4](0.5.0-rc3.md) (published as v0.5.0-dev4; its notes still carry the rc3 name), and everything those notes say still applies unless it's listed here.

## What's new

### Pictures and short clips

- **Ask about a picture.** Click the paperclip in Ask, drag a picture onto it, or paste one with ⌘V, then ask your question (or just press Return to hear what's in it). Wisp reads text in flyers, screenshots, receipts and whiteboards.
- **Turn a flyer into an event.** If the picture shows a dated event or to-do, Wisp offers to add it with the usual card. Nothing is added until you tap it, and words written in the picture can't make Wisp do anything else.
- **Short clips, up to 30 seconds.** Wisp looks at up to 8 still frames from the clip and listens to its sound. It sees sampled frames and hears the audio; it doesn't follow motion between them. Longer clips are refused with a short message.
- **Explain my screen.** Type `/screen` to show Wisp what's on your display. It only appears if you have already given Wisp Screen Recording (in **Settings ▸ Privacy & Access ▸ More Access…**); Wisp never asks for it on its own. You see a preview of the capture first and can throw it away with one tap.
- **Your pictures stay with you.** Wisp keeps no copy of the picture, and the picture itself never leaves your Mac. Your chat keeps your question, a note that a picture was attached, and Wisp's answer. That answer describes the picture, so it's in the chat like any other reply (and a cloud model you've turned on can see the chat).

### Fixes

- **Report a Problem saves in front.** Saving a report from the notch opens the save dialog above the notch instead of behind it.
- **Mail suggestions catch more.** Emails whose plain-text part is empty are read from their HTML, times like "6–7:30 PM" no longer confuse the date, "3 days left" is understood, and cancelled invites no longer become reminders.
- **Setup and Ask.** When Wisp becomes ready with notifications off, a quiet dot on the notch says so. The goal suggestion checks again on every open whether a cloud model is on. The Brief's Gmail reconnect link opens Wisp's Settings. A reply in the Wisp window that couldn't read a source offers **Allow in Wisp…**, and "What should I prep for…" is suggested only for your own events.

## Known issues

- Pictures and clips work only with a model on this Mac that can see (Wisp's own Gemma is the one we tested). With a cloud or other model, Wisp says it can't read pictures.
- Wisp sees a picture at about 280 tokens' worth of detail, so very small print on a large screenshot or a dense page may be missed.
- In a clip, Wisp sees up to 8 still frames, not motion, and it may guess which words go with which frame.
- Attaching in the Wisp window's Chat is still Debug Mode only; use the notch.
- Drag, paste and `/screen` are new and haven't been tried widely; tell us if one doesn't pick up your picture.
- A chat reopened from history loses its **Allow in Wisp…** button.
