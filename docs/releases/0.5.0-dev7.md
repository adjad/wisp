**Wisp 0.5.0-dev7** is a development build for testers. It builds on [dev6](0.5.0-dev6.md), and everything those notes say still applies unless it's listed here.

## What's new

### Save power on battery

- **Lighter on battery.** When your Mac is running on its battery, Wisp holds the graphics chip to 15 W while it writes a chat reply or updates your Brief. Replies start a little later (about a quarter of a second on a typical answer) and type out about 5% slower, for about a third less graphics power. Plugged in, nothing changes.
- **Only while it's working.** The limit is on only while that reply or Brief item is being written, and is gone as soon as it's done.
- **Still easy while you're away.** After 10 minutes away from your Mac, background work keeps running at 10 W, as in dev6.
- **Research and everything else you start run at full power.** So does everything while another app (a game, a render, an export) is busy on the graphics chip. The Daily Summary doesn't use the model, so it isn't affected.
- **One switch.** The notch's quick settings switch is now called **Save power**. It's on by default; turn it off and Wisp never limits power.

## Known issues

- If Wisp is using a separate oMLX app rather than its own engine, a limit stays on for about 15 seconds after the reply finishes.
- After you unplug, it can take up to about a minute before replies are limited.
