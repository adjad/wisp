**Wisp 0.5.0 dev1** is a development preview. It catches up on your mail, texts, calendar and reminders before you ask, and it can now add the things you missed to your calendar for you. Everything still runs on your Mac.

> This is a pre-release built for testing. It is ad-hoc signed (not notarized), so macOS will ask you to confirm the first time you open it: right-click Wisp.app ▸ Open.

## Before you ask (proactive cards)

The Brief now opens with up to five **Before you ask** cards. Each card is something Wisp already looked into, with one button to finish it.

- **Reads all your sources.** Wisp goes through your recent email, texts, Notes, calendar and reminders, summarizes each conversation, and picks the few things that actually need you.
- **Only messages that need something from you.** A text or email becomes a card when it asks you to do something *and* you haven't read it yet. If you've read it, it becomes a card only once it has sat unanswered for a day; until then it's just a to-do. Small talk ("lol") never becomes a card.
- **One-tap actions.** A card can draft a reply for you to edit and send (email or text), set a reminder, add an event, hold time on your calendar to work on something, or open the original message. Nothing is ever sent until you tap.
- **Security alerts come first.** Account and security email gets its own card at the top, with a safe title. This covers exposed secrets (like GitHub's "Possible valid secrets detected"), new sign-ins, password resets, failed payments and locked accounts. Codes, tokens and links are never shown.
- **School mail is no longer missed.** Canvas, Ed and Gradescope notices (quizzes, exams, graded work, new assignments) now reach your cards. A Canvas digest is named by the next thing actually due, and a due-date change replaces the original date.
- **Better reminder times.** A deadline with a time ("HW3 due Mon 11:59 PM") gets two reminders, at 9 AM that day and 3 hours before. Wisp never offers a reminder for a time that has already passed.
- **Holds time to work.** For homework or a project due soon, Wisp finds a real free slot on your calendar and offers to block it. It doesn't do this for things you simply attend, like a review session.
- **Analyze now and hourly refresh.** Tap Analyze now to refresh on the spot. Otherwise the whole Brief (cards, to-dos and summaries) regenerates about once an hour while your Mac is idle, and never while you're using Ask.
- **Dismiss anything.** Tap × on a card and it stays gone.

## Automatically added to Calendar and Reminders (new)

When a text or email has a firm date and you haven't acted on it, Wisp adds it for you and then tells you on a card marked **ADDED**, with **Undo**.

- Events with a start time (exams, appointments, interviews, recitals) go on your calendar. Deadlines ("HW4 due Friday 11:59 PM", "pay by Nov 3") become reminders.
- It's deliberately careful. It only acts on:
  - a saved contact, someone you've written to, or someone at your own school or organization;
  - an explicit date, worked out from when the message arrived;
  - a time at least 30 minutes away;
  - something not already on your calendar.
- It adds at most 3 per refresh and 10 per day.
- **Undo** removes exactly what Wisp created, and that thread is never added again.
- Turn it off with `proactive_auto_add: false`, and the same items come back as tap-to-add cards instead.

## Brief and Day

- **Easier to read.** A one-line summary of your day, then the cards, then a to-do list built from every source (each item shows where it came from), then per-conversation summaries you can expand.
- **Needs you** lists the most important things today, with clear tags (OVERDUE, DUE, ALERT, MAIL, TEXT). Promotional senders no longer take a slot.
- **Clear items from Needs you.** Hide a reminder or any other item from the list without deleting it from Reminders.
- **Reply to email from the Brief.** Wisp drafts the reply, you edit it, and you tap Send.
- **Day** places timed to-dos on the timeline and lists the rest under "Still to fit".
- **Account filter.** Switch the Brief between all accounts, your own, or a linked Google account.
- **No more blank Brief.** The "Wisp's service isn't running yet" error and the empty tab are fixed. The Brief loads more than twice as fast, keeps showing the last good version while it refreshes, and retries on its own.

## Connectors

- **Google Workspace.** One Google sign-in per account now feeds both the Brief and the Workspace tools. That covers about a week of mail, calendar events and past meetings to follow up on, with a per-account switch.
- **Disconnect & erase.** This removes everything Wisp stored for that account (mail, events, summaries and learned routines) and never touches your own texts, Notes or Reminders.
- **Texts from unsaved numbers.** A friend texting from a new number is read and shown as the formatted number. Only automated-looking senders (short codes, links, opt-out wording) are skipped.
- **MCP.** Servers with very large tool lists, such as Google Workspace, now connect.
- **Email summaries** label each linked Google account separately.

## Models and Ask

- **Gemma 4 support.** Wisp runs on Gemma 4 E4B locally.
- **Cloud models (optional).** There's a Google Gemini preset under Settings ▸ Cloud inference, plus an **Everything** option that runs all of Wisp on the cloud model. It handles Gemini's tool-call format, its rate limits (with automatic retries) and Gemma's inline thinking. Background refreshes pause on a cloud model, so your questions keep the rate limit.
- **Model-only mode (opt-in).** Every Ask turn can go straight to the model (`model_only: true`). This build adds:
  - better answers to "what should I do today?";
  - writing requests answered directly;
  - no invented personal facts;
  - personal questions re-checked against your own data instead of the web.
- "What should I do today" and "plan my day" now use the Brief's own day plan.

## Safety and privacy

- Nothing is sent without your tap. The only thing Wisp does on its own is add calendar events and reminders, and only within the limits above.
- Card and Undo actions can't be triggered from a shell command.
- Conversations that contain real secrets (codes, tokens, passwords) are never read by the model.
- Everything runs locally unless you turn on a cloud model.

## Known limits

- Ad-hoc signed preview, not notarized. Apple Silicon only; requires macOS 14 or newer.
- The model engine is not bundled in this preview: install oMLX (0.7 or newer, macOS 26+) and load a model such as Gemma 4 E4B before opening Wisp.
- Auto-added events don't yet carry the source message in the event's notes; it's shown on the card instead.
- Model-only mode is off by default in this build.
