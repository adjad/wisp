**Wisp 0.5.0-dev6** is a development build for testers. It builds on [dev5](0.5.0-dev5.md), and everything those notes say still applies unless it's listed here.

## What's new

### Documents and PDFs

- **Ask about a document.** Attach a PDF, Word (.docx or .doc), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), RTF, OpenDocument text, a web page (.html) or a plain-text file (.txt, .md, .csv, .json) the same way as a picture: the paperclip, drag it onto Ask, or paste it. Ask your question, or just press Return for a summary.
- **Pictures in any format your Mac opens.** PNG, HEIC, JPEG, screenshots, TIFF, GIF and more all work; Wisp turns them into a small JPEG before reading.
- **Scanned PDFs.** A PDF with no text in it (a scan or a photo) is read from its first 4 pages as pictures.
- **Invitations become events.** If the document is an invitation, ticket or notice with a date, Wisp offers to add it with the usual card. Nothing is added until you tap it, and text inside the document can't make Wisp do anything else.
- **Your documents stay with you.** Wisp keeps no copy of the document, and the document itself never leaves your Mac. Your chat keeps your question, a note that a document was attached, and Wisp's answer.

## Known issues

- Documents up to 20 MB. Wisp reads about the first 24,000 characters of a long document (roughly 10 pages) and says when it stopped early.
- Old Excel (.xls) and PowerPoint (.ppt) files can't be read; save them as .xlsx or .pptx. Password-protected PDFs can't be read. Pages (.pages) and Numbers files aren't supported yet.
- A scanned PDF is read from its first 4 pages only, and small print may be missed.
- A large Word or PowerPoint file with embedded fonts or embedded objects may be refused as too large to open safely, even when its text is short. Saving a copy without embedded fonts usually helps.
