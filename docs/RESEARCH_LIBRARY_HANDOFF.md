## Selection and plan

The audit ranked (1) reopening saved research, (2) app/backend recovery, and
(3) reliable settings-save feedback. Saved research is selected because Wisp
already retains reports, plans, evidence, and pins, but the app offers no way
to return to a previous job after another job or an app restart.

Deliver a menu-accessible Research Library with search and recent/pinned/status
filters, the 30 latest jobs plus all pinned jobs, and opening into the existing
main-app research experience. Opening must never start research automatically.
Saved reports retain citations, draft plans remain editable, active jobs reconnect,
and paused jobs resume only through the explicit Resume control.

Own `AppDelegate.swift`, `ResearchModel.swift`, the research controls in
`ResearchView.swift`, new library Swift files, `service/research/store.py`, and
dedicated tests/scripts/docs. Resume also owns the small `ResearchManager.start`
change in `service/research/orchestrator.py`: unpause an existing worker or
recreate a lost worker through one operation. No changes to `service/main.py`, chat/memory/email
plumbing, design artifacts, or Smart Search. No integration dependency on those
workstreams.

Implementation:

1. Add a library transport/model and list view, including empty/error/retry states.
2. Include pinned jobs beyond the recent-list limit without changing the API shape.
3. Wire the menu to saved-job opening; restore persisted phases and reconnect streams.
4. Scope asynchronous callbacks/actions to their selected job. Resume works with
   both an existing paused worker and a worker lost after backend restart.
5. Verify offline through synthetic HTTP/state fixtures, real store fixtures, native
   Swift state tests, and a full Swift build. Record the final handoff below.

All validation uses temporary state and inert transports. No live reports,
credentials, models, source apps, real sending, or application installation.

## Delivered behavior

Right-click Wisp's menu-bar icon and choose **Research Library…**. Search by
title/question, filter Recent / Pinned / Unfinished, and select a job to open it
in the existing main-app research panel. The library refreshes whenever opened;
failed refreshes retain previous rows and offer Retry. All pinned jobs remain
listed even after they fall outside the 30 most recent jobs.

Opening reads saved state only. Reports retain Markdown and source citations;
plans reopen for review; active jobs attach after the saved event cursor;
paused jobs expose Resume. Connections reconcile with saved snapshots and retry
with bounded backoff; replayed events do not double-count evidence. Unique
selection/connection identities reject old responses and stream callbacks.

Explicit Resume uses one backend Start operation, which now unpauses an existing
worker or creates a missing worker. Startup turns interrupted plan generation
into a deterministic draft for review. Cancel can finish a recovered paused job
even without a worker. Stale controls cannot revive terminal jobs. Drafted
steering stays with its job, failed deletion leaves the job open, and navigating
away while a plan save is pending prevents its delayed Start action.
